# Garage-to-ADU Conversion: The #1 Home Improvement Megatrend of 2026

Converting an underutilized garage into an **Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU)** or guest house can add usable living space. Its financial outcome depends on the municipality, lot status, construction scope, rental demand, taxes, and permits; a single ROI or property-value increase cannot be transferred between regions or countries.

An existing garage can avoid some foundation and shell work compared with a ground-up addition, but moisture control, insulation, door-opening infill, utilities, and approvals can materially change the budget and schedule.

This comprehensive guide covers everything you need to know: spatial planning, insulation requirements, plumbing retrofits, and cost breakdowns.

## Why a Garage Conversion Outperforms Building Ground-Up

Unlike a traditional room addition, a garage usually already supplies a slab, exterior framing, and a roof structure. Reusing them can reduce some shell work, but it does not guarantee a fixed saving: slab moisture, insulation, door-opening infill, utilities, and approvals may be substantial costs.

### Key Strategic Benefits:
1. **Flexible Use Potential:** The space may work for family, work, or rental use, but any rental income should be calculated from local market data, taxes, and rental rules.
2. **Multigenerational Living:** Provides an independent suite for aging parents or adult children seeking private living options close to home.
3. **Utility Proximity:** Attached garages share a wall with the main home, placing main electrical panels and plumbing stacks just feet away.

## Core Engineering Steps: From Concrete Shell to Living Space

Converting a cold garage requires resolving three structural challenges: **slab moisture mitigation, wall insulation, and door opening infill**.

![Job site installation detail: Wall framing insulation layers](/images/posts/garage-adu-conversion-2026/insulation-step.jpg "Construction detail: Wall thermal insulation layer assembly featuring 2x6 framing, rigid XPS boards, and vapor barrier")

### 1. Concrete Slab Preparation & Insulation
Garage slabs are typically uninsulated, non-level, and poured lower than the main home subfloor.
- **Moisture Barrier:** Lay down a heavy 10-mil (250 micron) polyethylene vapor barrier over the existing raw concrete.
- **Rigid Insulation:** Install 2-inch (50 mm) rigid foam board (XPS or PIR) providing minimum R-10 thermal resistance.
- **Subfloor Layer:** Top with interlocking subfloor panels (like DRIcore) or 3/4-inch (19 mm) tongue-and-groove plywood before laying waterproof SPC vinyl plank flooring.

### 2. Exterior Wall & Ceiling Insulation
Unheated garage walls require full thermal envelope upgrading to meet modern energy codes (R-13 to R-21 in US climate zones 3–5 / 100 mm to 150 mm mineral wool in Europe).
- **Framing Infill:** Fur out existing 2x4 (38 x 89 mm) studs to 2x6 (38 x 140 mm) or install continuous exterior insulation.
- **Air Sealing:** Spray foam or acoustic sealant applied to all top plates, sill plates, and electrical cutouts.

### 3. Garage Door Opening Infill
Demolish overhead tracks and build a weather-tight 2x6 framed wood stud wall across the garage door opening, flashed properly to withstand driven rain.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Sacrificing Headroom:** Verify the required finished ceiling height with the local code and project; there is no single value that applies everywhere.
2. **Inadequate HVAC:** Garages require dedicated climate control. Ductless mini-split heat pumps (12,000 BTU) are the gold standard for quiet heating and cooling.
3. **Zoning & Permit Oversights:** Always verify local municipal codes for parking replacement requirements and setback clearances before beginning demolition.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Do I need a permit to convert a garage into an ADU?
Often. The required permit, reviews, and inspections depend on the municipality, lot status, planned use, and scope of structural and utility work. Confirm the requirements with the local building authority before design or demolition.

### How long does a garage-to-ADU conversion take?
A professional build typically takes **6 to 10 weeks** from slab preparation to final inspection, while DIY projects average 3 to 5 months depending on trades availability.

### How do you add plumbing to a garage concrete slab?
Plumbers either saw-cut a shallow trench into the existing concrete slab for gravity drain pipes (1/4 inch per foot slope) or install a macerating upflush pump system (like Saniflo) to avoid cutting concrete.
