## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Will a Matter smart home continue working if the internet drops?
Yes! Matter standard devices communication over Thread or Wi-Fi executes automations locally on your local home network. Motion triggers, lighting scenes, and automatic water shutoff function seamlessly without active cloud connectivity.

### Do I have to replace my existing decorative light switches?
No. Compact in-wall smart relay modules fit behind your standard existing mechanical switch plates inside the wall junction box, letting you retain physical switches while adding app and voice scheduling.

### How much electrical power does a smart home hub consume?
Modern Matter hubs and Thread border routers draw between 2 and 5 Watts—costing under $0.50 per month in electricity while helping save $15 to $30 monthly through automated energy management.

Building a smart home in 2026 is a straightforward, budget-conscious strategy to improve everyday comfort, fortify home security, and cut monthly utility expenditures.
