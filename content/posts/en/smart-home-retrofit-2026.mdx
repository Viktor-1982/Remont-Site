## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Will a Matter smart home continue working if the internet drops?
Matter can communicate locally without internet access, but whether a particular automation continues to work depends on its controller, device transport, and whether it relies on the manufacturer's cloud. Before buying, verify which functions remain local and how the system behaves during power or internet outages.

### Do I have to replace my existing decorative light switches?
No. Compact in-wall smart relay modules fit behind your standard existing mechanical switch plates inside the wall junction box, letting you retain physical switches while adding app and voice scheduling.

### How much electrical power does a smart home hub consume?
Hub power draw and possible energy savings are product- and household-specific. Compare the measured standby consumption of the equipment with your tariff, then verify any savings against utility data rather than assuming a fixed monthly amount.
